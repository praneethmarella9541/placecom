# Delete Your Account

**Last updated:** September 6, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of your The Nucleus account (package `in.thenucleus.app`) and associated personal data, operated by Prachemur Labs LLP.

## How to request deletion

Send an email to [info@thenucleus.in](mailto:info@thenucleus.in) from your registered email address with the subject line:

**"Account Deletion Request"**

Please include your registered email address and, if known, your role (Admin / User / Staff), so we can locate your account quickly.

We will confirm receipt within 2 business days and remove your personal account data from our production systems within 7 business days of your request.

## What gets deleted

- Your account credentials and login access
- Your profile information (name, email, phone number, role)
- Your personal push-notification device tokens
- Personal interaction history tied solely to you as a user of the app

As described in our [Privacy Policy](https://www.thenucleus.in/privacy), removal from routine backups follows a separate cycle and completes within 90 days of account closure. The 7-business-day period above covers removal from our live, production systems; it does not guarantee immediate removal from backup copies.

## What may be retained

The Nucleus is a business tool. Some data you created or handled belongs to Prachemur Labs LLP's customer organization as business records rather than to you personally, and may be retained (reassigned to another team member, anonymized, or kept for legitimate business and legal purposes) even after your individual account is deleted. This includes:

- CRM, candidate, or lead records entered while using the app
- Call, SMS, WhatsApp, and email communication logs sent or received on behalf of the organization
- Meeting recordings, transcripts, and summaries shared with your team
- Records we are required to keep for accounting, legal, security, or regulatory compliance

## Questions

Contact us at [info@thenucleus.in](mailto:info@thenucleus.in) with any questions about this process or to check on the status of a deletion request. See our [Privacy Policy](https://www.thenucleus.in/privacy) for more on how we handle data.
