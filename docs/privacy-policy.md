## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains what information The Nucleus collects, how we use
and protect it, and the choices you have. It applies to your use of the Service
at https://thenucleus.in and all related applications.

The Nucleus is a unified workspace and CRM platform that connects to your Google
Workspace / Gmail account (with your explicit consent) to let you manage email,
files, calendar, contacts, forms, meetings, and customer relationships from one
place. It also provides optional communication features (SMS, WhatsApp, voice
calls) and AI-assisted features.

By using the Service you agree to this Policy. If you do not agree, do not use
the Service.

## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information you provide
- **Account details:** name, email address, password (stored only as a secure
  hash), organization/team information.
- **Content you create in the App:** CRM leads and companies, notes, form
  definitions, broadcast lists, and messages you compose.

### 2.2 Google user data (accessed only with your consent)
When you connect your Google account, we request the following OAuth scopes and
access the corresponding data **only to provide features you actively use**:

| Google scope | What it lets the App access | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|
| `userinfo.email` | Your Google email address | To identify your account |
| `gmail.readonly` | Read your Gmail messages and metadata | Display your inbox, search, and CRM email activity |
| `gmail.modify` | Read, label, and modify Gmail messages | Manage labels, mark read, organize mail |
| `gmail.send` | Send email on your behalf | Send replies and compose mail from the App |
| `https://mail.google.com/` | Full Gmail access | Draft management and message operations not covered by the scopes above |
| `drive` | View and manage your Google Drive files | The in-app Drive browser: upload, move, share, preview |
| `calendar.readonly` | Read your calendar events and availability | Show your calendar and free/busy times |
| `calendar.events` | Create and manage calendar events | Schedule meetings and send invitations |
| `contacts` | Read and manage your contacts | Contact book and syncing contacts you create |
| `contacts.other.readonly` | Read "other" contacts | Autocomplete recipients you've interacted with |
| `forms.body` | Create and edit Google Forms | The in-app Forms editor |
| `forms.responses.readonly` | Read Google Forms responses | Display and export form responses |

We **do not** request or access Google data beyond these scopes, and we access
data only when you use the corresponding feature.

### 2.3 Communication data
If you use SMS, WhatsApp, or calling features, we process phone numbers, message
content, call metadata, recordings, and transcriptions to deliver those
features.

### 2.4 Automatically collected data
Standard technical data such as IP address, browser/device type, and log data
used for security, debugging, and service reliability.

- We use Google user data **only to provide or improve user-facing features**

- Humans do **not** read your Google user data except: (a) with your explicit

## 5. AI-Assisted Features and Third-Party Processing

Some optional features (the Mail Assistant and call transcription) process your
content using a third-party AI provider, **OpenAI**, to generate the result you
requested (for example, answering a question about your emails or transcribing a
call recording).

- Content is sent to OpenAI **only when you actively invoke** the relevant
  feature, and **only the content needed** to fulfil that request.
- We use OpenAI's API, under terms where **your data is not used to train
  OpenAI's models**.
- This processing is solely to provide the user-facing feature you invoked, in
  line with the Limited Use requirements in Section 4.

You can choose not to use these AI features; the rest of the App works without
them.
